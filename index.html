Delayed task alerts

When you open the app, any tasks past their due date (and not complete) are flagged. You can draft a reminder email, CC'd to a manager, for each affected teammate.

Default email template

Used when you click the ✉ icon next to a teammate on the roster. Use {name} for their name. Use {week_range} for the current week's date range, {tasks_by_status} for a count per status (e.g. "🟩 Completed: 10"), and {week_tasks} for a full day-by-day list of this week's tasks — each day's date is bolded and followed by that day's attendance (e.g. Office/WFH/Leave/Rest Day) — all pulled live from your saved schedule data, no manual typing needed. Email bodies can't carry real font color or bold, so those are approximated with colored emoji (🟢 on time, 🔴 delayed, plus a color per status/attendance) and Unicode bold characters for the date.
